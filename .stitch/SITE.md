# AGENT AMAR - Site Constitution & Roadmap

## 1. Core Identity
- **Project Name:** AGENT AMAR
- **Stitch Project ID:** 17648819819210029385
- **Mission:** Autonomous AI-powered mail intelligence and inbox management mobile application for students and young professionals that cuts through inbox chaos, extracts actionable deadlines, and proactively reminds users of critical opportunities.
- **Target Audience:** College students, interns, new grads, and young professionals juggling internship applications, placement drives, assignment deadlines, and faculty communications.
- **Voice & Persona:** Quietly intelligent, proactive, crisp, supportive, hyper-focused on signal over noise.

## 2. Visual Language
- **Primary Vibe:** Intelligent, Youthful, Atmospheric Dark Navy, Cyber-Tactile Minimalist.
- **Key Visual Elements:**
  - Dark Navy canvas (#1B3C53) with layered elevation cards in deep oceanic blues (#234C6A).
  - Warm beige (#D2C1B6) as the signature AI accent color.
  - Live AI pulsating status indicators & real-time countdown clocks in Geist Mono.
  - Glass-morphism and glowing neon/ambient accents for critical items.
  - Clean non-corporate typography pairing (Bricolage Grotesque + Geist + Geist Mono).
